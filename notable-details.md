##  Tech Stack & Tools
* **Data Visualization:** Tableau Public
* **Data Processing:** Python (Pandas, NumPy)
* **Storage:** Google Drive / CSV

##  Key Insights & Features
* **Attrition Trends:** Analyzes age-based and demographic drop-off rates.
* **Financial Risk:** Segments students by financial risk categories to predict potential attrition.
* **Marketing ROI:** Evaluates the effectiveness of student acquisition channels.
